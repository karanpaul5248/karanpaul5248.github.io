Punjabi wedding photographer in Saskatchewan.

KaranKreation photographs and films Punjabi weddings across Saskatchewan — mehndi, sangeet, Anand Karaj, ceremony and reception, with photo and video together from one filmmaker who understands the celebration from the inside.

A Punjabi wedding is loud, warm and full — big families, long days, and moments that happen fast. One filmmaker who stays with your family through every event keeps the story whole, from the first mehndi to the last dance.

Filming since 2017. Karan has been behind the camera for nine years — photo, video and drone together from one filmmaker, not a crew handed off to strangers.
