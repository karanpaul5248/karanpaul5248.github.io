KaranKreation is Karan — one wedding photographer and filmmaker in Saskatoon, Saskatchewan. The work started in 2017, with videography, and nine years later it is still one person behind the camera: photo, video and drone together, from the first enquiry to the finished photos and film.

The website is new. The experience is not. Weddings across Saskatchewan and Canada, Anand Karaj celebrations, elopements, destination weddings and cinematic music videos — all made by the same person, with calm direction, candid moments and films that feel like the day felt.
